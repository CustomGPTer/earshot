# Earshot

Earshot is an Android app for podcasts. Over 19,000 come inside it, sorted into 111 lists by category, and it
can search and add any other public podcast. It has no adverts in the app, no accounts and no paid version.

- Website: https://customgpter.github.io/earshot/
- Latest app file: https://github.com/CustomGPTer/earshot/releases/latest
- Privacy: https://customgpter.github.io/earshot/privacy.html

## Good to know before you start

- **Playing uses mobile data unless you download.** Tapping an episode plays it over the internet, at about
  30 to 60 MB an hour. Download on Wi-Fi with the arrow beside an episode to listen without data.
- **Downloads are tidied up for you.** A download is deleted a day after you finish the episode, and only
  the newest five are kept for each podcast. Both rules can be changed or switched off in Settings.
- **The built-in list is for finding podcasts.** It can be browsed with no signal; opening a podcast and
  playing an episode needs the internet unless the episode is downloaded.
- **Android Auto hides apps that do not come from Google Play.** In Android Auto's settings tap Version ten
  times, then tick Unknown sources under Developer settings.
- **If the sound stops with the screen off**, set Earshot's battery use to Unrestricted in the phone's
  settings.

## What it does

- 19,312 podcasts built in: the UK top 200, and the top 200 for each of 19 categories and 91 sub-categories
- Today's live chart for the UK or any of 48 countries, and a search of Apple's public podcast directory
- Your own podcasts by feed link or Apple Podcasts page link, shared from the browser, or imported as an
  OPML list from another app or a backup file from Airwave
- Home: continue listening, then the newest episodes from every podcast you have added
- Speed from 0.8x to 3x, for every podcast or for one; trim silence; voice boost
- Skip the start and end of each episode, for each podcast; skip buttons with amounts you choose
- Full show notes with links and timestamps you can tap, and chapters where a podcast has them
- Bookmarks with notes, and a queue you can drag into order
- Downloads that tidy themselves, new-episode notices and optional automatic downloads
- Newest or oldest first, hide played, mark all as played and search, for each podcast
- Sleep timer, lock-screen controls, Bluetooth and headset buttons, Android Auto, casting and a widget
- An equaliser, a backup file with an automatic copy kept on the phone, and a Help page inside the app

## What it cannot do

Earshot is a player. It does not make, host or change any podcast, and it cannot find or remove adverts
inside an episode. A podcast that is only available inside one company's own app has no public feed and
cannot be played. Video podcasts play as sound only. It is new; if something does not work on your phone,
please open an issue.

## Where the listings come from

The built-in lists, the chart and search use Apple's public podcast directory. Episodes, show notes and
chapters come from each podcast's own feed. Earshot is not connected with Apple or with any podcast or
publisher; podcast names and artwork belong to their owners.
