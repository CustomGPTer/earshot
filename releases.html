Release notes

What changed in each version of Earshot. The newest is at the top.

Version 1.0 18 November 2026Latest

  • 19,312 podcasts built in, in 111 lists: the UK top 200, and the top 200 for each of 19 categories and 91 sub-categories. The lists open with no signal.
  • Today’s live chart for the United Kingdom or any of 48 countries, by category and sub-category.
  • One search box that looks through the built-in podcasts as you type, then the whole of Apple’s public podcast directory.
  • Your own podcasts: a feed link, the address of a podcast’s page on Apple Podcasts, a website that points to its own feed, or a link shared to Earshot from another app.
  • Bringing a list over: an OPML file from another podcast app, or a backup file from Airwave, which brings your place in each episode and your queue as well. Your list can be saved as an OPML file.
  • A Home tab with Continue listening and the newest episodes from every podcast you have added, with Hide played.
  • A Library tab with your podcasts, the queue, downloads and bookmarks.
  • Playback speed from 0.8× to 3×, for every podcast or for one. Trim silence and voice boost.
  • Skipping a set number of seconds at the start and the end of each episode, for each podcast.
  • Skip-back and skip-forward buttons with amounts you choose, used by headset, car and widget buttons too.
  • Full show notes with links and timestamps you can tap. Chapters from a podcast’s chapter list, from the sound file, or worked out from timestamps in the show notes, with previous and next chapter on the player.
  • Bookmarks with a note.
  • A queue that can be dragged into order, with Play next and Add to end of queue.
  • Episode downloads, on Wi-Fi only unless you change it, with new episodes downloaded automatically if you ask for that. A finished download is deleted after a day and only the newest five are kept for each podcast; both can be changed or switched off.
  • For each podcast: newest or oldest first, Hide played, Mark all as played, and a search of its episodes.
  • A remembered place in every episode, and a notice when a new episode is out.
  • A sleep timer with fade-out, or stopping at the end of the episode.
  • Lock-screen and notification controls, Bluetooth and headset buttons, Android Auto, casting, and a home-screen widget.
  • An equaliser with ready-made settings, and bass boost.
  • A backup file for podcasts, places, the queue, bookmarks and settings, with an automatic copy in the phone’s Documents folder.
  • Light, dark and true-black themes, three tile sizes, and a player that takes its colour from the artwork.
  • A Help page inside the app, and a notice when a newer version is ready.
  • Video podcasts play as sound only.
  • Trim silence, voice boost and the equaliser do not apply while casting, and a downloaded episode is cast from the internet, not from the phone.
  • Android Auto shows Earshot only after Unknown sources is ticked in Android Auto’s developer settings.
  • Casting needs Google Play services on the phone.
  • The built-in lists are the United Kingdom’s. The country button changes today’s chart and search, not the built-in lists.

Checking your download

The file for version 1.0 is called Earshot.apk. It is 7 MB (6,969,306 bytes). Its SHA-256 checksum is:

d51cfe9cf3fa1399d270fd8d5a385db1dd777842ac684097eaa298e5be8cb6f8

On Windows, run certutil -hashfile Earshot.apk SHA256 and compare. Every version is signed with the same key, so each one installs over the last and keeps what you have saved.

Older versions are on the GitHub releases page.
