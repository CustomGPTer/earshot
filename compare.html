What Earshot does not do

Earshot has no radio, no audiobooks, and no syncing between devices. It is not on Google Play, so you install it from this site and it tells you when a new version is out.

Where the others do more

Podcast Addict also plays live radio and audiobooks and imports and exports OPML files. Pocket Casts works on Chromecast, Wear OS and Sonos and syncs across devices. Both are on Google Play.
