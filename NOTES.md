First release of Earshot, a podcast app for Android.

- 19,312 podcasts built in, in 111 lists by category and sub-category, that open with no signal
- Today's live chart for the UK or any of 48 countries, and a search of Apple's public podcast directory
- Add your own podcasts by link, or bring a list over from another podcast app or from Airwave
- Speed for every podcast or for one, trim silence, voice boost, and skipping the start and end of episodes
- Show notes with links and timestamps you can tap, chapters, bookmarks and a queue you can reorder
- Downloads that tidy themselves, a sleep timer, Bluetooth, Android Auto, casting, a widget and a backup file
- No adverts in the app, no accounts, no tracking

**Good to know:** tapping an episode plays it over the internet, so download on Wi-Fi to listen without data. A download is deleted a day after you finish the episode and only the newest five are kept for each podcast; both can be changed in Settings. See https://customgpter.github.io/earshot/#first

Install steps and full notes: https://customgpter.github.io/earshot/releases.html
